REEL & RHYTHM — COMPLETE WEBSITE BUILD PROMPT

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a premium, modern, editorial, data-driven media and intelligence platform called Reel & Rhythm (R&R).

Brand positioning

Reel & Rhythm is a data-driven media, research, and intelligence platform focused on the African entertainment and creative economy.

The platform covers the business and performance side of African entertainment rather than celebrity gossip or conventional entertainment news.

The core areas include:

African film

Television

Brand tagline

Core products

The website must be built around four major proprietary products:

R&R Brief — flagship weekly newsletter

R&R Insights — editorial and research platform

R&R Charts & Rankings — recurring entertainment rankings and data products

R&R Reports — deeper research reports

This should feel like a serious media intelligence and research company, not a celebrity blog.
